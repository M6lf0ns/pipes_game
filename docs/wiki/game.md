# How to play my game 

Pipes is a 2D environmental puzzle platformer where you play as a robot navigating an abandoned factory. Use your surroundings to outsmart security bots and find your way to freedom.


1. Input
- A/D - move left/right
- W/S - climb up/down ladders
- A/D + LEFT CTRL - sprint
- A/D + LEFT SHIFT - push/pull boxes
- E - pick up/drop throwable pipe
- F - open vents/turn on speakers/rotate mirrorpipes
- LMB while holding pipe - aim 
- RMB while aiming - throw pipe
- SPACE - jump

2. Core Mechanics
- Core mechanics of the game are:
- Moving boxes to open doors/block enemies
- Using speakers to stun bots
- Blocking/Unblocking mirrors or mirrorpipes to kill laser enemies
- Throwing throwable pipes/dropping them through vents to make the dog open doors for you
- Rotating mirrorpipes to redirect laserbeams
